About Mera Sai Tours & Travels

At Mera Sai Tours & Travels, we are committed to turning every journey into a memorable experience. With years of trusted service in local and outstation travel, we have proudly served over 25000+ satisfied clients, helping families, groups, and individual travelers explore with comfort, safety, and ease.

Our team believes in providing reliable, professional, and customer-focused travel solutions — whether it’s a family outing, corporate trip, wedding transportation, school tour, or customized travel plan. We offer a wide range of services including car and bus rentals, outstation packages, corporate travel support, and special event transport, ensuring personalized attention and seamless travel from start to finish.

Ujjain Mahakaleshwar Jyotirling

Experience the divine charm of Ujjain with a comprehensive guided tour, perfect for families, pilgrims, and spiritual travelers. Travel in comfort with AC bus or private car options, with fixed and well-organized pickup from Mumbai. The journey begins from a designated pickup point in Mumbai, ensuring a smooth, safe, and timely departure for all travelers.

Explore the sacred city of Ujjain, one of the seven Moksha-puris of India, and seek blessings at the world-famous Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Feel the powerful spiritual energy as you attend the divine Bhasma Aarti (subject to availability) and witness centuries-old rituals performed with devotion.

Shirdi Sai Baba Darshan

Experience the divine presence of Shri Sai Baba at the sacred Shirdi Samadhi Mandir. This serene and spiritually uplifting darshan image captures the beautifully adorned idol of Sai Baba seated on a golden throne, surrounded by intricate temple carvings and vibrant floral decorations.

Shirdi is one of India’s most visited pilgrimage destinations, attracting millions of devotees seeking peace, faith, and blessings. Through our specially curated Shirdi tour packages, Mera Sai Travels ensures a comfortable and well-organized spiritual journey for families, senior citizens, and devotees.
